# ObjectForge Scope 2 — Goal-Directed Functional Construction, Revision 2

## Why Scope 2 was revised

The first Scope 2 implementation proved architecture selection and functional recovery, but its generated forms did not consistently meet the standalone visual and construction threshold established by Scopes 0 and 1. Functional coverage was incorrectly allowed to stand in for finished-object quality.

Revision 2 keeps the functional planner and adds a separate blocking **close-inspection completion gate**. An asset now fails even when it satisfies every requested function if its proportions, joinery, controls, secondary forms, material separation, underside, or back remain under-resolved.

## Objective

Construct detailed standalone models from functional requirements rather than a named object class.

The planner receives goals such as:

- remain freestanding and stable;
- support or contain a declared load;
- provide repeated access;
- direct emitted energy;
- organize repeated items visibly;
- remain portable;
- fit a bounded footprint.

It compares eight bounded architecture alternatives, selects one by weighted requirement coverage, risk, complexity, and constraints, then composes the selected result from the registered support, shell, articulation, repetition, joinery, detail, and material grammars.

## Fixed benchmark briefs

1. Adjustable directional energy above a work surface.
2. Protected transport with repeated access and internal organization.
3. Elevated load support with open storage and cable routing.
4. Portable visible organization for six retained instruments.

None of the briefs contains an object-class field.

## Revised visual and construction gates

Each generated object must now contain at least:

- 48 separately inspectable geometry components;
- 14 semantic part classes;
- six differentiated PBR material classes;
- 18 secondary or tertiary detail components;
- a completed underside or back surface;
- functional controls, closures, handles, or organization affordances;
- one explicit `refinement.verify_close_inspection` receipt.

The benchmark additionally requires coherent proportions and visible manufactured relationships: fastened joints, supported transitions, retained clearances, complete edges, and no reliance on a distant hero camera to conceal unfinished regions.

## Required evidence

Every benchmark retains:

- the original functional brief;
- all scored architecture alternatives;
- the selected functional architecture;
- requirement-by-requirement coverage;
- explicit construction and refinement operations;
- a canonical standalone GLB;
- a distinct showcase GLB and inspection viewer;
- embedded procedural PBR textures;
- animation and physics contracts where movement applies;
- a failed construction alternative;
- comparison of at least two repairs;
- a preserved checkpoint and one selected repair;
- close-inspection quality metrics;
- reopen validation and a hash-linked manifest.

## Completion boundary

Scope 2 Revision 2 proves bounded goal-directed architecture selection, detailed first-party construction, and functional recovery. It does not claim unrestricted natural-language invention, unbounded architecture search, manufacturing certification, or automatic engineering approval.
