# ObjectForge Scope 2 — Goal-Directed Functional Construction

## Objective

Advance ObjectForge from named object-family plans to construction driven by functional requirements.

Scope 2 does not receive an object class. It receives goals such as:

- remain freestanding and stable;
- support or contain a declared load;
- provide repeated access;
- direct emitted energy;
- organize repeated items visibly;
- remain portable;
- fit a bounded footprint.

The planner compares bounded architecture alternatives, selects one by weighted requirement coverage, risk, complexity, and constraints, then composes the existing support, shell, articulation, repetition, joinery, detail, and material grammars.

## Fixed benchmark briefs

1. Adjustable directional energy above a work surface.
2. Protected transport with repeated access and internal organization.
3. Elevated load support with open storage and cable routing.
4. Portable visible organization for six retained instruments.

None of the four briefs contains an object-class field.

## Required evidence

Every benchmark must retain:

- the original functional brief;
- all scored architecture alternatives;
- the selected functional architecture;
- requirement-by-requirement coverage;
- explicit construction operations;
- a canonical standalone GLB;
- a distinct showcase GLB and inspection viewer;
- PBR materials and embedded procedural textures;
- animation and physics contracts where movement applies;
- a failed construction alternative;
- comparison of at least two repairs;
- a preserved checkpoint and one selected repair;
- reopen validation and hash-linked delivery manifest.

## Completion boundary

Scope 2 proves bounded goal-directed selection and functional recovery. It does not yet claim unrestricted natural-language object invention, unbounded architecture search, manufacturing certification, or automatic engineering sign-off.
