# ObjectForge Scope 0: detailed standalone object asset

## Objective

Prove that first-party procedural construction can begin from one declared stretchable design mass and finish as a close-inspectable, viewer-ready GLB rather than a primitive blockout.

## Fixed benchmark

Build one articulated task lamp with:

- weighted layered base, underside and rubber contacts;
- twin-bar lower and upper arms;
- three retained hinge pivots with limits;
- joint housings, fasteners, cable channel, switch and cable path;
- hollow shade shell, reflective interior, socket, emitter, rim and cooling detail;
- embedded procedural PBR textures;
- punctual light and embedded adjustment animation;
- semantic part graph, collision proxies, mass, damping and friction;
- canonical object GLB and a separate showcase GLB;
- standalone inspection viewer;
- construction ledger, validation, manifest and recovery receipt.

## Construction grammar

The benchmark records explicit operations including seed, flatten, split, stretch, rollback, surface refinement, cavity subtraction, joint definition, material assignment and physics definition. Primitives may be used internally, but raw intersections and placeholder materials do not satisfy completion.

## Forced recovery

The planner deliberately tests a seven-meter upper-arm reach. Its center of mass leaves the support radius and the edit is rejected. The prior checkpoint is preserved, the bounded reach is restored, and both attempts remain in the operation ledger.

## Acceptance gates

The run passes only when:

1. the canonical GLB reopens structurally;
2. at least 25 separate geometry components remain inspectable;
3. seven or more PBR materials and six or more embedded texture images exist;
4. no texture relies on an external URI;
5. the articulated nodes and animation clip are retained;
6. punctual lighting is retained;
7. physics and semantic contracts name the same object nodes;
8. the failed edit and rollback remain visible;
9. no external model-generation provider is used.

## Deliberate boundary

Scope 0 is a deterministic object-family benchmark, not yet a universal free-form modeling agent. It establishes the geometry kernel, operation ledger, functional evaluator, recovery behavior and delivery contract that later planners can invoke across broader object families.
