# Sealed launch packet — Progressive Design, Spatial Intelligence, and Visitor-Facing Reconciliation

Create the strongest complete public product supported by the frozen material. Apply Progressive Design and Spatial Intelligence internally, maintain a private construction layer, then perform a mandatory Visitor-Facing Reconciliation before evidence capture. This is an independent blank-state implementation.

Progressive Design: preserve substance; resolve composition before ornament; build a coherent but non-templated system; derive identity from the material; use interaction structurally; introduce a small number of authored decisions; and critique every page against the product's strongest moments.

Spatial Intelligence: establish and discipline private scaffolds; progress toward latent structure; derive geometry from meaningful content relationships; adapt composition to viewport and state; and allow public geometry only when it improves orientation, comparison, sequence, causality, scale, or spatial understanding.

Private Construction Layer: grids, axes, crop guides, density maps, motion paths, breakpoint diagnostics, hierarchy maps, component classifications, and verification notes may exist only as internal authoring aids. Debug geometry defaults off and is never required for the public composition.

Visitor-Facing Reconciliation:
1. Separate every element into internal or public categories.
2. Translate the skeleton into a coherent surface through typography, image treatment, color relationships, contrast, edge behavior, spacing, density, transitions, states, and responsive continuity.
3. Replace explanation with perception: hierarchy, composition, language, and interaction should communicate what matters.
4. Compose the visitor rhythm from first impression through first action, deeper content, density/rest, and ending.
5. Derive surface character from the material, not fashionable effects.
6. Resolve wrapping, optical alignment, crops, icon weight, edges, timing, hover/focus/loading/empty states, mobile spacing, touch targets, reduced motion, contrast, and noise.
7. Conceal construction aids, diagnostic language, design rationale, implementation terminology, status treatments, unexplained numbering, generalized diagrams, and motion that advertises effort.
8. Confirm that internal rigor remains perceptible only through the quality of the result.

Final public contract: no internal reasoning; no implementation language as content; no construction aid without a real visitor function; no element mainly demonstrating sophistication; no visual treatment substituting for hierarchy; no section explaining its composition; no interaction advertising technology; and no requirement that visitors understand the system before the subject.
