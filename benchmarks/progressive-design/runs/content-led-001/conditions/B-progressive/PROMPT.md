# Sealed launch packet — Progressive Design

Create the strongest complete public product supported by the frozen material. Apply the following sequence without turning it into visible process language:

1. **Integrity:** preserve real content and function; remove filler, fabrication, repetition, broken states, and false product value.
2. **Composition:** resolve hierarchy, scale, proportion, density, negative space, alignment, sequence, focal movement, section relationships, and responsive pacing. Do not solve weak composition with decoration.
3. **System:** establish coherent typography, spacing, image treatment, color behavior, component relationships, responsive rules, and interaction states without flattening the product into sameness or mechanical cards.
4. **Identity:** derive variation and character from the actual subject, imagery, function, tone, and structure rather than arbitrary theming.
5. **Interaction:** use interaction and motion to reveal hierarchy, connect states, preserve orientation, or strengthen transitions; never as spectacle or delay.
6. **Authorship:** make a small number of consequential, deliberate exceptions where restraint, asymmetry, unusual crop, selective density, silence, or tension makes the work more truthful.
7. **Critique and elevation:** judge the product against its own strongest moments and continue until quality variation is intentional rather than accidental.
